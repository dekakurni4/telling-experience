The Magic Verb Shifter (Present → Past / Verb 2)

Untuk menceritakan pengalaman lampau, kita mengubah kata kerja (Verb 1) menjadi Verb 2 (Past Form). Ada yang cukup ditambah -ed (Regular), dan ada yang berubah unik (Irregular)!

Regular Verbs (Tinggal +ed)

Easy!
visit (mengunjungi)
play (bermain)
cook (memasak)
watch (menonton)

Irregular Verbs (Bentuk Berubah)

Special!
go (pergi)
see (melihat)
eat (makan)
buy (membeli)

The 3-Step Recipe to Tell a Great Story!

Seperti resep kue, cerita pengalaman (Recount Text) terdiri dari 3 bagian penting:

Langkah 1

1. Orientation (Pengenalan)

Jawab pertanyaan dasar: Siapa (Who), Kapan (When), dan Di mana (Where)?

"Last Sunday, my family and I went to Parangtritis beach."
Langkah 2

2. Events (Rangkaian Kegiatan)

Ceritakan kegiatannya memakai kata sambung urutan: First, Next, Then, Finally.

"First, we built sandcastles. Next, we drank fresh coconut water."
Langkah 3

3. Re-orientation (Penutup)

Bagikan bagaimana perasaanmu: "I was so happy!" / "It was unforgettable!"

"We went home in the afternoon. I was tired but very excited!"
📖 Contoh Cerita Lengkap

"A Wonderful Day at the Beach"

🚗🌊
Step 1: Orientation

Last Sunday, my family and I went to the beach. We went there by car. The weather was sunny and warm.

🏰🥥
Step 2: Events

First, my brother and I built a big sandcastle. Next, we swam in the refreshing sea. Then, we ate fried rice and drank fresh coconut water.

🌅😊
Step 3: Re-orientation

In the afternoon, we saw a gorgeous sunset and went home. I was tired, but I felt really joyful!
